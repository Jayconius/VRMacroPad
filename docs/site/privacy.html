VR Macro Pad

Privacy Policy

VR Macro Pad · Home

VR Macro Pad ("the app") is a program that runs on your own Windows PC. This policy explains what the app does with information when you connect it to your accounts, such as your Google / YouTube account.

The short version

  • The app runs on your computer. It has no accounts, no analytics and no server that collects your information.
  • I, the developer, do not receive, see, store or sell any of your data, including your Google or YouTube data.
  • Connecting YouTube is optional, you choose to do it, and you can undo it at any time.

What the app accesses when you connect YouTube

When you press "Connect YouTube account", you sign in on Google's own page and choose whether to allow access. If you allow it, the app is given a permission to use the YouTube Data API on your behalf (the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl). It uses that to:

  • read the name and statistics of your own channel (subscriber, view and video counts),
  • find your current or upcoming live broadcast, and read its title, visibility, viewer count, like count and stream health,
  • send a message to the live chat of your own broadcast, when you press a button that does that,
  • start or end your broadcast, start an ad break, and change the title, description, category or visibility of your own broadcast, when you press a button that does that.

The app only does these things when you press a button, or to show a status screen that you placed on your pad. It does not read other people's chat messages, and it does not act on any other person's account.

Where the information goes

Requests to YouTube are made directly from your computer to Google. The sign-in token that Google gives the app is stored on your computer, in the app's own data folder (encrypted with your Windows account where Windows supports that). It is never sent to me or to anyone other than Google. Information shown on your pad, such as your viewer count, stays on your computer.

Google API Services User Data Policy

VR Macro Pad's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your YouTube account is used only to provide the features described above. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not shared with anyone, and not used to train AI models.

Stopping and deleting

  • In the app, choose "Disconnect" on the YouTube card in Settings. This deletes the stored token from your computer.
  • You can also remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder (%APPDATA%\VR Macro Pad) removes everything it stored.

Other services

The app can also connect to other services you choose to use, such as Twitch, Kick, Discord and OBS. Each works the same way: it talks directly from your computer to that service using your own sign-in, and nothing is sent to me.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes
