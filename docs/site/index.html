Everything on one pad

Press a button and it does something. Buttons light up to match reality: your mic is muted, you are recording, chat is in emote-only mode. No scripting needed.

🎙️

Real macros

Keyboard shortcuts, sequences with delays, audio and mic controls, app launching and more, chained as steps on one button.

📺

Live mini screens

Clock, timers, dice, the song playing, your headset and tracker batteries, stream status and viewer counts, right on the pad.

💡

Buttons that know the state

Colour, label and animation change with the real state of your mic, your stream or your player, so you always see what is on.

🖼️

Make it yours

Any colour, your own pictures and GIFs, button effects like pulse, glow and heartbeat, and pages for every situation.

📐

Any size grid

Set the number of rows and columns per page, and have the pad fit any window, from a small strip to a full wall of buttons.

🧩

Plugins

Every service is a plugin you can switch on or off, and you can write your own. Each one keeps its own settings and login.

A real SteamVR overlay

Grab the pad, move it, resize it and pin it in the room or to your wrist, all from inside VR. Edit everything without taking the headset off.

  • Float it in front of you, in the room, or on your wrist.
  • Only when you look at it, if you would rather it stay out of the way.
  • Edit inside VR with SteamVR's keyboard.
  • No VR? It works just as well on the desktop.

Buttons only, when you want

One key and the window frame disappears, leaving just your buttons floating over your game, your stream layout or your desktop. Press it again to bring the controls back.

  • Not in the way: clicking the pad never steals focus from your game.
  • Always on top, if you want it that way.
  • Start with no window and use it only in VR from the tray.

Made for streamers

Streaming plugins put the things you reach for mid-stream on a button. Each one signs in with your own account, only ever acts on your own channel, and does nothing until you press a button or put a status screen on your pad.

💜 Twitch

  • Chat messages and announcements
  • Emote-only, followers-only, slow mode and shield mode
  • Ads: run one, snooze one, and an ad timer
  • Clips, stream markers, raids and shoutouts
  • Change your title and category

🟢 Kick

  • Chat messages
  • Change your title and category
  • Run an ad break, and see how many are left
  • Live status and viewer count

▶️ YouTube

  • Send a message to the live chat of your own stream
  • Go live and end your stream
  • Run an ad break
  • Change the title, description, category or visibility (public, unlisted, private) of your own stream
  • Live status, viewers, likes, stream health, and your subscriber and view counts

🤖 Streamer.bot & Discord

  • Run any of your Streamer.bot actions from a button
  • Post "I'm live" messages and screenshots to Discord
  • Discord mute, deafen and push-to-talk buttons
  • Show who last messaged you on Discord

YouTube and your data. When you connect YouTube, you sign in on Google's own page and choose whether to allow access. VR Macro Pad uses that access only to do what is listed above, only for your own channel, and only when you press a button or place a status screen. Everything runs on your own computer: nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone but YouTube. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Plugins for everything

Every service in VR Macro Pad is a plugin: a folder you can switch on, off or add. Each plugin brings its own buttons, mini screens and a settings card with its own login.

  • Built in: OBS, VRChat, SteamVR, Voicemeeter, Spotify, YouTube Music, Twitch, Kick, YouTube, Discord, Streamer.bot and a starter pack.
  • Write your own with a simple guide and a worked example.
  • Nothing else touched: a plugin that fails to load never stops the rest.
